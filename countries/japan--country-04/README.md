# Japan

Perception and motion planning in factory processes.

2027 edition · October 2026 assessment · text revision v1.1, 12 October 2026 · record `country:04` · rank 4.

## Assessment

Japan brings perception and motion generation into established manufacturing processes, building on robotics and precision-control capabilities.

## Evidence scope

## Evidence behind the assessment
