# South Korea

Robot coordination, learned control and research commercialization.

2027 edition · October 2026 assessment · text revision v1.1, 12 October 2026 · record `country:03` · rank 3.

Also indexed as: Republic of Korea, Korea.

## Assessment

Korea combines research investment with NAVER's cloud-based robot coordination, KAIST's learned locomotion control and university-led robotics commercialization.

## Evidence scope

NAVER's internal operations; KAIST's July 2026 HOUND experiments and February 2026 report on technology-transfer and investment activity. The March 2026 testbed demonstration and planned external access retain separate stages. Economy-wide research indicators provide context.

## Evidence behind the assessment

KAIST reported APT-RL control of HOUND on an indoor obstacle course and outdoor campus and forest terrain. [country:03:S12](https://www.kaist.ac.kr/newsen/html/news/?mode=V&mng_no=64450) Published 2026-07-16.

Physical research experiments; not evidence of customer deployment.
