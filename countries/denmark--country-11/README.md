# 🇩🇰 Denmark

Mobile logistics and collaborative-robot platforms.

2027 edition · October 2026 assessment · text revision v1.1, 12 October 2026 · record `country:11` · rank 11.

## Assessment

Denmark supplies AMRs for transport and collaborative robots for AI greenhouse harvesting, with named customer tasks.

## Evidence scope

UR and MiR platform supply, Odense development infrastructure, DTI incubation and Danish industry records. National turnover and local cluster activity retain separate scopes.

## Evidence behind the assessment

MiR describes three MiR250 robots moving containers between BORG Automotive’s warehouse and production areas through MES and fleet-software integration. [country:11:S1](https://mobile-industrial-robots.com/cases/borg-automotive) Publication date not recorded.

A platform-supplier case; this source does not establish that the customer site is in Denmark.
