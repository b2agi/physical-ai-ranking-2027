# B2AGI Physical AI 2027: Country Atlas Top 20

B2AGI examines how Physical AI moves from demonstration to deployment. This Country Atlas compares 20 countries and economies through their technical capabilities, customer applications, capital, operating responsibility and investment in future capacity.

2027 edition · October 2026 assessment baseline · source revision 2026-10-11 · English.

The order is based on qualitative comparison, not a composite numerical score. Record IDs remain stable and do not indicate rank. Each profile includes its assessment rationale, evidence scope and original source references.
