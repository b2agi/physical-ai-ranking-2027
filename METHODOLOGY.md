# Methodology

## What We Select

B2AGI Physical AI Ranking 2027 examines how intelligence reaches physical work. The geographic releases contain 20 countries and economies and 20 industrial clusters from the 2027 edition.

Physical AI connects perception, learning, planning or adaptive control to action in a physical environment. Components, development tools, operators, capital providers and institutions enter the selection through a documented contribution to that chain. Each rationale identifies the contribution being recognized.

## Five Analytical Lenses

| Lens | Assessment question | Evidence we examine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intelligence | What does the system perceive, learn, plan or control? | Technical papers, model and software releases, control methods and their demonstrated tasks |
| Physical | What work reaches a robot, component or customer site? | Physical tests, customer trials, integration records and operating reports |
| Capital | How is development or deployment funded? | Financing, customer contracts, capital-market access and financial statements |
| Trust | Who defines and checks the operating conditions? | Supervision, test protocols, certificates, standards and regulatory decisions |
| Beyond | How does the ecosystem invest in its next generation of capabilities? | R&D, education and fixed-capital investment, together with attributable research, training and industrial infrastructure |

B2AGI applies these five lenses to compare industrialization pathways. The assessment combines documented activities with quantitative indicators in their original units and periods. It uses qualitative comparison rather than a composite numerical score.

## Geographic Order

The assessment prioritizes Intelligence and Beyond: the capacity to develop intelligent physical systems and the investment in knowledge, people and industrial infrastructure that carries that capacity forward. Physical applications, capital access and operating responsibility establish how those capabilities reach practical work. Robot production and installation volume are supporting indicators.

Industrial-robot installations, robot density and R&D expenditure were examined alongside operating reports, technical research and institutional records. Each statistic retains its observation year, unit and geographic definition. The source references attached to each entry let readers examine the basis of the assessment.

### Reading the Leading Countries

The United States leads through computing and robotics platforms that other developers use, university research and shared development tools. China follows through sensing and robot development connected to electronics engineering, manufacturing and shared training facilities. The comparison recognizes different strengths: the US profile has broader reusable development platforms; China's profile connects component engineering to product development and industrial demand.

South Korea's case combines investment in research with several distinct technical contributions: NAVER's cloud-based robot coordination, KAIST's learned locomotion control and university-led technology commercialization. WIPO's 2026 human-capital-and-research pillar places Korea first, Germany third, Sweden fifth, Japan eighteenth and Israel nineteenth. These economy-wide indicators provide context for the robotics cases. Korea's 2024 R&D expenditure was 5.13% of GDP, compared with Japan's 3.62% and Germany's 3.13%.

### Beyond: Investment in Future Capacity

Beyond examines investment in knowledge, people and the industrial foundation for the next generation of physical intelligence. The country assessment uses R&D expenditure, government education expenditure and gross fixed capital formation, each expressed as a share of GDP. Regional assessment uses attributable research, training and development infrastructure within each cluster's boundary.

The reference calculation covers the original 24 candidate economies. Within the 2019-2023 observation window, 2021 provides complete three-indicator coverage for 19 economies, the largest coverage and the latest year at that coverage level. The reference equals the mean of three normalized ratios: each observed value divided by the highest observed value among the 24 candidates, multiplied by 100. The 2021 denominators are 5.75725% for R&D, 7.60224008560181% for education and 41.3521203793076% for fixed capital. A dash records a missing observation; a reference is calculated when all three observations are present.

The table reports the preserved 2021 WDI snapshot. It describes economy-wide investment intensity. The country assessment reads that investment alongside the attributed intelligence, products, customer work and infrastructure in each profile. Finland, India, Saudi Arabia and Turkey are comparison candidates outside the selected 20.

The Beyond reference is the equal-weight mean of three normalized, economy-wide expenditure shares. It provides context for the qualitative assessment; the B2AGI order comes from the comparative assessment of the country profiles. Government education expenditure and gross fixed capital formation describe broad investment in future capacity. Robotics training, venture funding and access to shared robotics facilities are assessed through their own attributed records.

The 2024 R&D observations in Country Context update one indicator. The 2021 reference combines three indicators observed in the same year. Substituting a 2024 R&D value while retaining 2021 education and capital values would produce a mixed-year calculation. A same-year update requires all three series for the candidate economies and new normalization denominators.

## Evidence and corrections

Evidence distinguishes research, simulation, physical demonstration, supervised trials, customer operations, financing and certification. A signed agreement retains its date and status; it is not automatically counted as a completed customer operation.

Statistics retain their observation year, unit, denominator and geographic definition. Corporate activity, financing and customer-site deployment are attributed separately. Reported outcomes remain attributed to the publisher making the report.

To request a correction, identify the record ID, the disputed statement and a dated primary source with an exact page or section. A rank change requires comparison with the relevant alternatives through the same five lenses. Factual corrections and changes to the ranking are recorded separately.

Record IDs stay stable when the order changes. Missing source access dates remain blank. The JSON preserves the original source references and related record IDs; Related country profiles are available in the country release; other related profiles remain outside the current geographic releases.

Send corrections to [aei.b2agi@gmail.com](mailto:aei.b2agi@gmail.com).

## Institution selection

Institutional entries identify research, testing, regulatory, industry-support and capital-market activities. Regulators and exchanges are assessed for their contribution to operating conditions and capital access.

Technical authorship, funding, standards administration and operating support retain separate attribution. When several participants share an output, each retains its contribution. The selected record identifies the activity being assessed.
