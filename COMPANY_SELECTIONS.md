# Company selection comparisons


Decision date: 2026-10-11. Each comparison identifies the selected activity, the alternative's strongest evidence and the reason for allocating the place. Current and Next describe the selected contribution's stage. Several operating suppliers give way to research programmes that add a different technical question to the edition.

### Perception-to-Action Research: Hugging Face / RealSense

N113 Hugging Face is selected for SmolVLA's compact vision-language-action model, community-data training and physical manipulation experiments. N063 RealSense contributes adopted RGB-D camera input to LimX's navigation demonstration; NVIDIA supplies the odometry and LimX integrates navigation. That camera contribution belongs with established supply. The emerging-research place goes to SmolVLA for its inspectable model experiments and task-adaptation resources.

RealSense retains supplier credit and its source reference under N042 LimX. C068 Orbbec supplies other depth-sensing coverage. The selection gives up RealSense's standalone corporate profile. SmolVLA adds a compact, community-data approach alongside the existing model researchers; joint authors, hardware designers and data contributors retain credit. I026's LeRobot entry concerns shared infrastructure, while the company entry concerns the developing model.

Sources: [SmolVLA paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.01844v1), June 2025, author affiliations and physical experiments; [Hugging Face research account](https://huggingface.co/blog/smolvla), 2025-06-03, architecture and SO100/SO101 evaluations; [model card](https://huggingface.co/lerobot/smolvla_base), intended use and adaptation; [RealSense navigation account](https://www.realsenseai.com/news-insights/insights/advancing-autonomous-humanoid-navigation-with-realsense-depth-cameras-and-nvidia-cuvslam/), camera contribution and LimX integration.

### Driverless Passenger Service: Waymo / May Mobility

C102 Waymo is selected for its documented rider-only service across five regions through June 2026 and published deployment conditions. C059 May Mobility's Peachtree Corners service is a concrete commercial driver-out operation with eight stops. The place goes to Waymo for the broader documented service footprint. May's service retains its operating status. Waymo's manufacturer recall is part of its operating evidence, not erased by selection.

Sources: [Waymo Safety Impact](https://www.waymo.com/safety/impact/), rider-only miles and city table through June 2026; [CPUC permit table](https://www.cpuc.ca.gov/regulatory-services/licensing/transportation-licensing-and-analysis-branch/autonomous-vehicle-programs/autonomous-vehicle-program-permits-issued), Waymo driverless deployment; [May Mobility](https://maymobility.com/posts/may-mobility-now-offers-autonomous-driverless-rides-in-peachtree-corners/), 2025-02-12, launch and service details; [Waymo recall](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2025/RCLRPT-25E034-2471.PDF), 2025-05-12, pp. 1-3.

Dobot's supplied research platform remains a substantive alternative; its Braille application is coverage lost in this choice. The welding method belongs to the external team.

Sources: [Royal IHC project account](https://www.robotminor.nl/pub/versatile-welding-cobot-royal-ihc), published 2025-03-16, fall 2023 project, Solution, Integration and Program; [Dobot Bristol case](https://www.dobot-robots.com/insights/case-studies/driving-innovation-at-bristol-robotics-laboratory.html), MG400/CR3 and Ao Li's research account, undated.
