# 🇨🇳 Shenzhen–Dongguan

Robot engineering, incubation and manufacturing access.

Full assessment region: Pearl River Delta Core — Shenzhen–Dongguan.

2027 edition · October 2026 assessment · first published 12 October 2026 · record `cluster:01` · rank 1.

## Assessment

Shenzhen and Dongguan connect sensor and robot development with engineering education, prototyping facilities and manufacturing access. XbotPark's university and startup programmes link talent development to product engineering.

## Geographic and evidence scope

Located development and incubation in Shenzhen and Dongguan; named XbotPark startup outcomes and HKUST's April 2026 educational visit. Shenzhen's 2024 academy incubation total covers hardware entrepreneurship. Overseas customers and Hong Kong educational origins retain their locations.
