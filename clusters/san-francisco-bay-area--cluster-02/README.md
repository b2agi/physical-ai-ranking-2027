# 🇺🇸 San Francisco Bay Area

## Assessment

The Bay Area combines model and robotics-platform development with Berkeley's robot-learning education and Stanford's shared physical research facilities. Reusable software and product companies connect research to physical systems.

## Geographic and evidence scope

Development across San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara and Alameda counties; Berkeley's Spring 2026 course and Stanford Robotics Center's physical research bays. Global platform adoption and site-specific operations retain separate geographic scopes.
