# Country comparison notes

Editorial review: 12 October 2026. These notes make the retained order easier to examine; they do not introduce scores or a new ranking calculation. The original assessment baseline remains October 2026.

The comparisons below identify the industrial contribution being weighed and a relevant competing pathway. They are B2AGI’s editorial judgments. Evidence stages and geographic limits remain attached to each country profile. A profile with narrower documentation is not proof that the country has weaker capabilities overall.

<a id="country-01"></a>

## 1. United States

The leading position combines reusable model and robotics platforms, named customer tasks and growth capital. China provides the closest manufacturing counter-case; hardware output alone does not determine this order.

[Assessment and original sources](countries/united-states--country-01/README.md)

<a id="country-02"></a>

## 2. China

China is the manufacturing counterweight to the United States: sensing, electronics and robot hardware share dense supply chains. Korea’s coordination and learned-control cases illustrate a different, narrower route into physical intelligence.

[Assessment and original sources](countries/china--country-02/README.md)

## 3. South Korea

The third position rests on the combination of operating coordination, physical learned-control experiments and research commercialization. Japan supplies a strong factory-engineering alternative; the detailed comparison remains in the methodology.

## 4. Japan

Japan places perception and motion generation inside established factory processes. Its shared computing and process engineering carry the close comparison with Germany, rather than production volume alone.

Switzerland contributes a focused research-to-product route through learned inspection control. France provides the adjacent general-purpose model and open-tool counter-case; the distinction is operational specialization versus broader development ambitions.
