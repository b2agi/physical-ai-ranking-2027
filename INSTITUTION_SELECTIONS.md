# Institution selection comparison

Decision date: 11 October 2026.

## Worker-safety evidence: NIOSH and A3


I054 NIOSH adds fatality research and joint analysis of injury claims alongside OSHA's guidance. A3 contributes engineering expertise to that shared guidance and has distinct standards work. The place goes to empirical worker-harm evidence to complement retained standards and guidance coverage. I002 (A3) was retired on 2026-10-11; its joint-guidance credit is retained in I054. The I054 source references identify the joint guidance and the two studies with their observation periods.
