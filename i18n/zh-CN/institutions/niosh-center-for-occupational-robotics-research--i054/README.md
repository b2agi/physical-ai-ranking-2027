# NIOSH / Center for Occupational Robotics Research

职业环境下的机器人危害研究与指导

`I054` · Institutions 50

## 评估与入选依据 · 英文原文

NIOSH contributed to joint workplace-robotics guidance and published fatality and nonfatal-injury research relevant to worker protection.

## 证据范围 · 英文原文

Joint guidance credits A3 engineers and NIOSH researchers. The 2023 fatality study covers 1992-2017; the 2026 NIOSH/Ohio study analyzes 1,076 claims from 2001-2020 and credits Ohio's data provision and the joint research team.
