# HAI Robotics

仓储机器人系统

`C048` · Current 100

## 评估与入选依据 · 英文原文

HAI Robotics supplies ACR and AMR warehouse systems, including a Hyundai Mobis application.

## 证据范围 · 英文原文

Hai Robotics' Hyundai Mobis case describes tote retrieval and parts transport at the Suwon High Tech Center's spare-parts warehouse. Picking workstations retain the human picking step. This record recognizes the warehouse-system contribution described in that case.
