# 권인소 / In So Kweon

DRC-HUBO 공동 연구 리더십

`P038` · Influence 50

## 선정·평가 근거 · 영문 원문

In So Kweon co-led Team KAIST with Jun-Ho Oh and Rainbow researchers at the 2015 DARPA Robotics Challenge. Their DRC-HUBO robot completed the competition’s eight tasks and won the finals.

## 근거 범위 · 영문 원문

KAIST’s June 2015 competition report identifies shared team leadership and the completed course. It attributes the result to the team rather than assigning individual vision or depth algorithms to Kweon.
