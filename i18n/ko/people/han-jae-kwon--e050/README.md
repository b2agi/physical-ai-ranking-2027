# 한재권 / Han Jae-kwon

## 선정·평가 근거 · 영문 원문

Han Jae-kwon and Lee Byung-joo delivered robotics lectures at Hanyang's August 2022 Robot Day.

## 근거 범위 · 영문 원문

Hanyang reports lectures, demonstrations and robot-making activities. The separate HERoEHS team paper credits Han with ALICE's mechanical design and Younseal Eum with exterior design.
