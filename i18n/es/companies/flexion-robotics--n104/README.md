# Flexion Robotics

Políticas de control para tareas de cuerpo completo

`N104` · Next 100

## Fundamento de selección y evaluación · original en inglés

Flexion’s June 2026 Reflect v1.0 report describes a humanoid retrieving a parcel, navigating between floors and interacting with doors and elevators. Learned motion policies and whole-body control connect those steps into a research demonstration.

## Alcance de la evidencia · original en inglés

Company-reported Reflect v1.0 research demonstration, published 29 June 2026; the entry covers the documented missions and controller. Customer acceptance and safety certification are outside this report’s evidence.
