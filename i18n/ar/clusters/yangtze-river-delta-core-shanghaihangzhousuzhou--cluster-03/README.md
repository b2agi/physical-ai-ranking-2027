# Yangtze River Delta Core — Shanghai–Hangzhou–Suzhou

<div dir="ltr" lang="en">

`cluster:03`

</div>

المرتبة: 3

## الدور · الإنجليزية

<div dir="ltr" lang="en">

Training infrastructure, robot products and physical research

</div>

## أساس الاختيار · الإنجليزية

<div dir="ltr" lang="en">

The cluster brings Shanghai’s training infrastructure and Hangzhou’s robots and physical-system research into one analytical region, tracing research into product development.

</div>

## نطاق الأدلة · الإنجليزية

<div dir="ltr" lang="en">

The analytical region covers Shanghai, Hangzhou and Suzhou. Cited robot manufacture, R&D, training and product development are located in Shanghai and Hangzhou; customer sites retain their locations.

</div>
