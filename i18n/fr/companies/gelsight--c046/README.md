# GelSight

Instrumentation de recherche tactile

`C046` · Current 100

## Motifs de sélection et d’évaluation · original anglais

GelSight supplies the Mini sensor used by Politecnico di Milano researchers to acquire tactile measurements on industrial components with a FANUC collaborative robot. The researchers retain the anomaly-detection methods and evaluation.

## Portée des éléments probants · original anglais

Research v1, 2026-08-31, sections 3.3 and 4: adopted Mini instrumentation for robot-mounted dataset acquisition and external inspection-method comparison.
