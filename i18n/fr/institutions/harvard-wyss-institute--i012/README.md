# Harvard Wyss Institute

Recherche et licences de robots d’assistance

`I012` · Institutions 50

## Motifs de sélection et d’évaluation · original anglais

Harvard Wyss and SEAS researchers developed personalized soft-exosuit assistance from muscle ultrasound and tested it across walking speeds and inclines. Wyss also records technology licensing to ReWalk.

## Portée des éléments probants · original anglais

Harvard’s November 2021 account documents joint Wyss and SEAS research and walking tests. The ReWalk licensing record covers a separate technology-transfer activity.
